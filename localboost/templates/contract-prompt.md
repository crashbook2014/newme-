=== STEP 1 — RESEARCH FIRST (mandatory, before drafting) ===

E-Commerce Law (نظام التجارة الإلكترونية) and its Implementing Regulations: what a service provider who sells online/through WhatsApp must disclose (identity, contact, price, total cost, terms), contract and invoice requirements, cancellation/return rights, advertising rules, and protection of customer data.
2. Electronic Transactions Law (نظام التعاملات الإلكترونية): whether an electronic signature, Nafath signature, or written approval on WhatsApp/email makes the contract binding and usable as evidence.
3. Civil Transactions Law (نظام المعاملات المدنية, 2023): formation of contracts, obligations of the parties, liability and compensation limits, termination, force majeure, and whether a liability cap and penalty clause are enforceable.
4. Freelance Document rules (وثيقة العمل الحر, HRSD / freelance.sa): whether the listed activities are permitted under the document, whether the document number must appear on the contract and invoices, and whether a commercial registration is needed.
5. ZATCA: the VAT registration threshold, whether a non-registered freelancer must state "not VAT registered" on invoices, and the e-invoicing (فاتورة) rules if registered.
6. Personal Data Protection Law (نظام حماية البيانات الشخصية) and SDAIA regulations: handling client data, customer photos, staff photos, consent, retention, and deletion.
7. Copyright Law (نظام حماية حقوق المؤلف): transfer of ownership of written content, designs, photos and the website.
8. Sharia-compliance points in Saudi contracts: avoid interest on late payment (use work suspension or a fixed, pre-agreed fee only if the research confirms it is acceptable), and check how penalty clauses (الشرط الجزائي) are treated.
9. Dispute resolution: competent courts in Riyadh, and whether amicable settlement first, then mediation (e.g. SCCA / Taradhi) can be written in.
