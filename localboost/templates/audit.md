# Audit Template & Scoring Rubric

Score **only what you can see**, and screenshot each finding into `LocalBoost/Leads/[Lead ID]/`. A low score means more need, which makes for a better lead.

## Rubric (total 100)

| # | Check | Points | How to score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Profile claimed / verified | 10 | 10 = "Own this business?" link absent and owner replies or posts visible · 0 = "Own this business?" visible |
| 2 | Correct primary category | 10 | 10 correct · 5 too generic · 0 wrong |
| 3 | Hours complete | 10 | 10 all days plus special hours · 5 regular hours only · 0 missing |
| 4 | Photos | 15 | 15 = 10+ recent, owner-uploaded, good quality · 8 = some · 0 = none or only customer photos |
| 5 | Description in Arabic, clear | 10 | 10 clear Arabic · 5 English only or vague · 0 none |
| 6 | Services / menu listed | 10 | 10 complete · 5 partial · 0 none |
| 7 | Contact: phone works and WhatsApp/website link | 10 | 10 both · 5 one · 0 none |
| 8 | Owner replies to reviews | 10 | 10 most replied · 5 some · 0 none |
| 9 | Website (mobile) | 15 | 15 fast, clear, WhatsApp button · 8 exists but weak · 0 none or broken |

**Qualified:** total ≤ 60, inside the target area, a target category, and open.
