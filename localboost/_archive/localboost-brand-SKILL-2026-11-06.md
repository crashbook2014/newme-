## Brand essence
LocalBoost helps local businesses improve their digital first impression and make it easier for customers to discover them, understand their services, and take the next step.

- **Positioning:** A practical, approachable digital-growth partner for local businesses in north Riyadh.
- **Brand promise:** Accurate listings, clear online information, and simpler customer enquiries.
- **Primary tagline (EN):** Findable. Clear. Ready for customers.
- **Primary tagline (AR):** سهل يلقونك. واضح. وجاهز لزبائنك.
- **Supporting campaign line:** Your next customer is looking nearby. Treat this as a creative line, never a guarantee of business results.

## Two service pillars
1. **Digital Presence** — findable on Maps, clear services, easy contact (profile + simple website).
2. **AI Business Assistance** — practical help with customer messages and feedback using **client-owned** tools. Approved approach (CEO 6 Nov 2026): **native Meta Business AI** on WhatsApp Business — **available now**, activated/configured from the Meta WhatsApp Business dashboard. LocalBoost sets up and configures; the client owns the account and pays platform/subscription fees. Do not sell custom agents beyond Meta Business AI; do not guarantee reply volume or sales.

## Core values
- **Clarity:** Make information easy to understand.
- **Practicality:** Solve real problems before adding complexity.
- **Trust:** Be honest about scope, costs, limits, and results.
- **Local understanding:** Respect local business realities and natural Saudi Arabic.

### Colors
- **Navy `#022754`:** Local, rocket body, headlines, navy grounds, tagline.
- **Orange `#EA5C0A`:** Boost, CTAs, accents, flame base.
- **Flame amber `#F6880F` / tip yellow `#FFD332`:** flame gradient only.
- **White `#FFFFFF` · Mist `#F2F2F2` · Ink `#0A1F3D`.**
- Do **not** use the old teal `#0BA9A6` as the primary accent anymore.

### Typography
- English: rounded geometric sans for wordmark (Nunito / Poppins energy); Inter or similar for UI/body (fallback Arial/Helvetica).
- Arabic: IBM Plex Sans Arabic or Noto Sans Arabic.
- Check Arabic shaping, spelling, and RTL. Short headings. Mobile-first.

### Layout
Generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, restrained cards, one primary CTA per section. Prefer real client-approved photography over generic stock. Arabic default for north Riyadh shop owners unless scoped English-only. Always pair brand lockups with AR tagline on Saudi customer-facing pieces.

### Logo rules
- Preserve proportions, clear space, and navy/orange relationships.
- Never stretch, rotate, add heavy shadows, recolor to teal, or rebuild from typed text alone.
- Prefer icon-only at tiny sizes; full lockup for letterhead, proposals, hero headers.

## Voice
Helpful, direct, grounded, locally aware, professional. Confident without hype.
- Short sentences; concrete outcomes (more walk-ins, clearer page, fewer repeated WhatsApp questions).
- Natural Saudi Arabic for outreach.
- Exact scope, exclusions, and what the customer must provide.
- Transparent on ongoing costs and third-party fees.
- No ranking / lead / booking / revenue guarantees.
- Never fabricate testimonials, reviews, client results, or business facts.
- Never imply affiliation with Google, Meta, or another platform.
- Outreach drafts signed «سعد من LocalBoost» until CEO says otherwise.

## Local and cultural context

- Verify hours, prices, addresses, services with owner-approved facts.

## CEO approval rules
Require CEO approval before: publishing price changes; new services; spend; legal changes; unsupported claims; material business-model changes; mass marketing; untested automations (Meta Business AI setup itself is approved and available now); sending any shop message; replacing the live logo asset.

Agents may draft, correct internal docs, prepare site/brochure copy, improve layouts, and run QA without publishing strategic changes.

## Production workflow (every deliverable)
1. Clarify audience, channel, objective, language, CTA.
2. Gather only owner-approved facts.
3. Draft in LocalBoost voice.
4. Verify names, numbers, prices, hours, addresses, links, spelling, RTL.
5. Apply rocket lockup + navy/orange palette (not teal monogram).
6. Test mobile, forms, calls, messages, booking flows.
7. Flag assumptions and third-party dependencies.
8. Present with clear scope and next step.

## Trust, privacy, and platform rules
- Explicit approval before editing profiles, sites, photos, or messaging flows.
- Client owns accounts; LocalBoost may be invited as manager. Never collect passwords or 2FA in chat.
- No fake reviews, spam, or impersonation.

## Default response format for LocalBoost work
- State the business problem.
- Give the practical improvement.
- Explain deliverables and boundaries.
- Use locked rocket branding when visual.
- End with one clear next step.
- Separate verified facts from assumptions.
