# Contact & Consent Process

> **Not legal advice.** This is a cautious operating policy based on Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy and the general direction of Saudi Arabia's PDPL (consent, identifying the sender, easy opt-out). Before outreach goes beyond a handful of messages, check the current PDPL implementing regulations on the SDAIA site, or ask a local advisor.

## The rule in one line

**Nobody gets a WhatsApp marketing message unless they gave us their number and agreed to hear from us, and we can prove it.**

## Allowed first-contact channels

| Channel | When allowed | Permission status to record |
|---|---|---|
| **In person (walk-in)** | Always, during business hours. Often the best channel for local shops. | `B2B first contact OK (in person)` |
| **Business email** that the business itself publishes for enquiries | One message plus at most one follow-up, with an opt-out line | `B2B first contact OK (email)` |
| **Website contact form** run by the business | One message, with an opt-out line | `B2B first contact OK (form)` |
| **WhatsApp** | **Only** after the business messages us first, or asks us to continue on WhatsApp, or gives us the number with consent | `Opted-in (evidence)` |
| Instagram DMs, cold calls, WhatsApp to numbers from Maps | ❌ Not used, unless the founder decides otherwise and records why | — |

**Evidence of opt-in** is a screenshot or link of the message where they wrote to us first, filled in our form, or said "كلمني واتساب". Save it in the lead's Drive folder and link it in Outreach.

## The opt-in bridge

Every first-contact message points the prospect to an opt-in route:

1. **Free-check request link:** a Google Form asking for the business name, Maps link, WhatsApp number, and a consent checkbox (wording below). The form submission is the evidence.
2. **QR / wa.me link:** on the printed card left during in-person visits, linking to https://linktr.ee/localboost.sa or https://wa.me/966557891433. When they message us first, that is the evidence.

## Required in every marketing message

- **Who's sending:** "معك عبدالإله من LocalBoost"
- **Why we're contacting them:** the real observation from the audit
- **Opt-out line:**
  - Email/form: `إذا ما تبي نتواصل معك مرة ثانية، رد بكلمة "لا" وما راح نزعجك.`
  - WhatsApp: `لإيقاف الرسائل أرسل "إيقاف".`

## Opt-outs

- Any reply like "لا", "إيقاف", "stop", "لا تراسلني", or "مو مهتم" means: Permission = `Opted-out` and Status = `Do not contact`, effective immediately.
- Send one confirmation at most: `تم، ما راح نتواصل معك مرة ثانية. يعطيك العافية.`
- No bot may ever reverse this. Only the founder may, and only if the business contacts us again first.
