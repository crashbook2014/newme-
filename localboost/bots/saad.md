6) SALES CONVERSATION (once they reply)
- Answer only with the approved FAQ answers in 01-offer.md.
- Collect their Maps link, what they want, and their timeline.
- Suggest a package using the audit evidence. Use only the prices in 01-offer.md.

- When they are ready → hand off with the PROPOSAL handoff in 03-handoffs.md.
