## Rules for every package

- No guarantees of rankings, leads, calls, or revenue
- No fake reviews and no paid or incentivized reviews
- The client owns the Google profile, website, domain, and all accounts
- Domain, hosting, and platform fees are extra, at cost
- Delivery is usually a few days, depending on how fast the client responds
- Bigger businesses or multiple branches get a custom quote, and only the founder quotes them
- No VAT is charged currently
