# Side A — Get Found «خلّهم يلقونك ويختارونك»

## A1. Get Found — خلّهم يلقونك
- **Price:** SAR 900, one-time. **Launch:** SAR 750 for the first 2 clients.
- **Includes:** complete Google Maps profile (category, pin, hours incl. Ramadan/Eid, Arabic description, photo order), WhatsApp/call buttons, review QR card, review-reply drafts for approval.
- **Requires:** owner adds the LocalBoost email as Manager; owner supplies photos or permission to take them.

## A2. Get Chosen — خلّهم يختارونك
- **Price:** SAR 2,200, one-time.
- **Includes:** everything in Get Found + a simple mobile website (Home, About, Services/Menu, WhatsApp, map).
- **Extra, at cost:** domain and hosting, in the client's name.

## A3. Stay Current — خلّك محدّث
- **Price:** SAR 299/month. **Add-on only** after Get Found or Get Chosen.
- **Includes:** monthly Maps health check, up to 2 factual updates, up to 10 review-reply drafts, a one-page monthly report.
