## What each service includes

**LB-GF · Get Found «خلّهم يلقونك»**
Complete Google Maps profile: correct category and pin, hours including Ramadan/Eid, Arabic description, photo order, WhatsApp/call/directions buttons, review QR card, and review-reply drafts for owner approval.
Requires: the owner adds the LocalBoost email as Manager, and supplies photos or permission to take them.

**LB-GC · Get Chosen «خلّهم يختارونك»**
Everything in Get Found + a simple mobile site (Home, About, Services/Menu with prices, map, WhatsApp button). Domain and hosting at cost, in the client's name.

**LB-SC · Stay Current «خلّك محدّث»**
Monthly profile check, 2 updates, 10 review-reply drafts, one-page report. Add-on only, after Get Found or Get Chosen.

**LB-CHK · Shop Check «زيارة التقييم»**
- Secret-shopper visit: welcome, wait, service, cleanliness
- Shop appearance: storefront, sign, display, menu, staff look
- Visible money leaks: waste, extra staff in quiet hours, missed sales
- Deliverables: photo report, score out of 100, top 5 fixes by priority, results session

**LB-MC · Monthly Care «المتابعة الشهرية»**
Monthly secret-shopper visit compared with last month, a check that the fixes held, simple checklists (opening, closing, cleaning, welcome), a short staff session, and a monthly report.

**Scope limits for the shop-visit side (LB-CHK, LB-MC):** no accounting, no sales or bank data, no supplier contracts, no profit promises.
