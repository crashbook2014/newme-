# LocalBoost — Visual Style ("Launch")

Every LocalBoost visual (website sections, posts, flyers, brochures, catalog images) uses the **launch** style.

## The scene
- **Night sky:** vertical gradient `#010d1f → #022754`, with a few small white stars.
- **Orange sunrise glow** rising from the bottom edge (orange `#EA5C0A` → amber `#F6880F` → soft yellow).
- **The rocket on a glowing moon:** use the rocket cut from the real logo (`localboost/site/assets/favicon.png`). Never redraw or retype it. It sits on a warm glowing moon with a **dashed yellow orbit ring** (`#FFD332`) around it.
- **Offers as launch stages:**
  - **١ · الانطلاقة** (Lift-off): «خلّهم يلقونك ويختارونك»: Get Found, Get Chosen, Stay Current
  - **٢ · المدار** (Orbit): «نشوف محلك بعين زبونك»: Shop Check, Monthly Care
- **The full logo on a white badge** (rounded white plate). Never place the logo directly on navy.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#022754` | Headlines, night sky, "Local" |
| Orange | `#EA5C0A` | Buttons, accents, sunrise, "Boost" |
| Amber | `#F6880F` | Sunrise and flame gradients |
| Yellow | `#FFD332` | Orbit ring, highlights on dark backgrounds |
| White / Mist | `#FFFFFF` / `#F2F2F2` | Cards, light sections |

**Never use teal**, the old accent colour.

## Fonts
- **Readex Pro**: headlines
- **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**: body text
- **Inter**: numbers and prices

## Icons
**No emojis** in product posts, catalog text or catalog images. Use the custom line icons (navy/orange, 2 px stroke, rounded) or illustrations in this style.

## Sizes
| Format | Size |
|---|---|
| TikTok / Story | 1080 × 1920 |
| WhatsApp / product post | 1080 × 1350 |
| Flyer / brochure | A4 |

## Labels that must appear when relevant
- Before/after examples: «مثال توضيحي»
- Money examples: «مثال حسابي، مو وعد» (the gain shown must be bigger than the fee)
- Prices: only 900 · 2,200 · 299/month · 3,300 · 1,700/month (Get Found launch 750 for the first 2 clients)
