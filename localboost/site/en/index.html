Questions we hear a lot

Do you guarantee the #1 spot?

We don't guarantee rankings or customer numbers — don't trust anyone who does. We guarantee your profile is complete, clear and easy to contact.

Do you write reviews?

Never. Fake reviews can get your profile removed. We give you a QR card that makes it easy for real customers to review you.

Whose name is the account in?

Yours — the Google profile, website and domain. We're only added as a manager, and you can remove us any time.

Do I need to give you my password?

Never. You add us as a manager from your phone, and we send you the steps.

How long does it take?

Usually a few days, depending on how quickly you send photos and details.

Can I get Stay Current on its own?

No. It's a monthly add-on after Get Found or Get Chosen.

Are you part of Google?

No. We're independent and not affiliated with Google or Meta.

What is a Shop Check?

We visit your shop as an ordinary customer, without your team knowing, and give you a photo report, a score out of 100, your top 5 fixes by priority, and a session to walk through the results.

Do you look at my accounts or sales?

No. We never ask for accounts, sales or bank data, and we never promise profit. We only work on what your customers see.
