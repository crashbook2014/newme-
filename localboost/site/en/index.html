FAQ

Common questions

Do I keep my accounts?

Yes, always. Your Google profile, website and domain are all in your name. We're added as a manager only — no passwords — and you can remove us any time.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. We don't guarantee rankings or customer numbers. We guarantee the work is complete, accurate and clear.

Is Meta Business AI available?

Yes. We set it up on your own WhatsApp Business account, which stays yours. Platform fees are paid by you; we only set up and configure.

Can I get Stay Current on its own?

No. It's only available after Get Found or Get Chosen.

What if I want to cancel?

Before we start: full refund. After we start: refund of the part not yet delivered. Third-party fees (domain, hosting, Meta) aren't refundable. Details in our policies.

Is there VAT?

No, we don't add VAT.
