Your first 3 photos are your new storefront

Customers rarely open the whole album. The first photos they see are what they judge you on, even if your shop looks better in person.

Do it today

  1. The storefront in daylight, from across the street, with the sign readable.
  2. The inside, tidy and well lit, before the rush.
  3. Your best-known product or service, close up and clear.

Avoid

  • Dark or blurry night photos
  • Customers' faces without permission
  • Your logo instead of a real photo of the shop

One more tip

Update your photos every month. Old photos tell customers: "nobody looks after this shop".
