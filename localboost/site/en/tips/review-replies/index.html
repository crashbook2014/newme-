Your review reply is read by the next customer

The reviewer has had their say. Your reply reaches the people still comparing you with someone else.

The formula

  1. Thank them and name the service they had.
  2. Explain or apologise, and say what you changed.
  3. Invite them back, without overdoing it.
Illustrative example

"Thank you for your note about the wait on Thursday. We've added a team member at peak time, and we'd be glad to see you again."

Avoid

  • Arguing with the reviewer
  • The same reply for everyone
  • Discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews
