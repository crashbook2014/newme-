Wrong hours lose you the customer twice

Once when they arrive and find you closed, and again when they write a review about the wasted trip.

Do it today

  1. Check your weekly hours day by day, especially Friday.
  2. Add "special hours" for Ramadan, Eid and National Day, a week before.
  3. Closed unexpectedly? Update it from your phone in a minute, before customers set off.

Avoid

  • "Open 24 hours" when you aren't
  • Different hours on Google, your sign and Instagram

Quick test

Ask a friend to search for your shop right now and read you what it says. If you're surprised, your customers will be too.
